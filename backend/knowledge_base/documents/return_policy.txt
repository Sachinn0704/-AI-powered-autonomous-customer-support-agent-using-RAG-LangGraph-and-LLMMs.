RESOLVEAI PRODUCT RETURN POLICY

1. Customers can request returns for delivered orders.
2. Return requests must be submitted within 30 days of delivery.
3. Customers must provide a valid order number.
4. Products may be returned for defects, damage or incorrect delivery.
5. Return eligibility is subject to verification.
6. Return requests are recorded in the system.
7. Return requests may require human approval.
8. Orders outside the return period are not eligible for return.
