RESOLVEAI REFUND POLICY

1. Refunds are available for delivered orders only.
2. The order payment must be completed.
3. Customers can request refunds within 30 days of delivery.
4. Orders outside the 30-day period are not eligible for automatic refunds.
5. Refund requests are subject to verification.
6. High-risk refund requests require human approval.
7. Refund requests may be rejected if the order does not satisfy the eligibility conditions.
8. Customers must provide a valid order number when requesting a refund.
